Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate? +
Any student currently enrolled in a recognized college or university in India. Register through your college SPOC.
Team size requirement? +
Teams can have 1 to 6 members. You can also participate as a solo hacker.
Is there a fee? +
No. Registration is completely free for all students and colleges.
Multiple teams per college? +
Yes. Multiple teams can be nominated through the SPOC portal.
What happens at Grand Finale? +
36-hour nonstop build sprint with blind judging on 5 criteria.
Need working prototype? +
Only if shortlisted for the Grand Finale. At registration, an idea PPT is enough.
How is judging done? +
Anonymized submissions, blind scoring by expert panel, algorithmic aggregation.
Prizes? +
Certificates, trophy and incubation support for winners. Prize pool will be announced soon.
